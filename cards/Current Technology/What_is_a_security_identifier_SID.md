What is a security identifier SID?

---

A unique value used in Windows to identify users, groups, and computer accounts consisting of 

Sausage (String) - The literal letter S at the start.
Rolls (Revision) - The version number (usually 1).
In  (Identifier Authority) - The issuing authority (e.g., 5 for NT).
Spicy (Sub-authorities) - The Domain/Machine identifier string.
Relish (Relative ID (RID)) - The specific account ID (e.g., 500).
